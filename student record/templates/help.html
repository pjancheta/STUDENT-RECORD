Quick help

A few helpful notes for everyday student record management.

Add a student

Choose Add new student in the sidebar. Student ID and email must each be unique. Required details are marked with an asterisk.

Create a record

Find and update records

Use the search box for a student name or ID, then combine gender, course, and year filters. Select the edit icon to update a record.

Open student records

Download a report

The reports page summarizes students by program and year. Export all student records to a spreadsheet-friendly CSV.

View reports

Keep your account safe

Change your administrator password from Settings. Use a unique password with at least 12 characters, uppercase and lowercase letters, a number, and a symbol.
